## Använd prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Create one polished high-fidelity mobile web app UI concept board, landscape, containing THREE large readable iPhone screen mockups side by side, all same coherent design. App is a private Swedish men's clothing discovery tool with Tinder-like product cards. This is a CONCEPT with fictional sample clothes and illustrative prices, not a live app. Minimal Scandinavian fashion editorial design, warm ivory backgrounds, charcoal typography, muted forest green save accent, restrained red sale labels, generous spacing, clear contemporary sans-serif typography. Beautiful believable studio product photography of men's clothes (navy overshirt, cream tee, grey jeans), no real retailer logos, no AI assistant UI. Small board title 'Hitta din stil'. Left phone caption '01 Upptäck': top app label 'Kläder', small filter control, 'Zalando · Herr', big navy overshirt product photo with three slim photo pagination bars, 1/3 indicator; below photo 'Overshirt', 'Exempelmärke', current price '499 kr', crossed old price '699 kr', clear small 'REA' badge. Bottom accessible large round undo, X and heart action buttons, text 'Hoppa över' and 'Spara', bottom nav Upptäck / Sparat / Hämta. Center phone caption '02 Produkt & bilder': same overshirt in another studio angle, gallery counter 2/3, back control, below 'Overshirt', material '100 % bomull', fit 'Regular fit', color 'Marinblå', large button 'Visa i butik'. Right phone caption '03 Sparade plagg': title 'Sparat', clean two-column wishlist grid of four men's clothing items with brand placeholder, price, heart icons and one REA label; bottom nav matching other two screens. All body copy in Swedish. Keep phones front-facing, no hands, no dramatic perspective, entire three screens visible with no cropping. UI should be practical for Safari on iPhone with bottom safe areas. Do not include reviews, AI recommendation controls, analytics or chat features. Small footer on concept board 'UI-koncept · exempelprodukter och exempelpriser'. Prioritize visual quality and legible concise labels.
